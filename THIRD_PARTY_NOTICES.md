# Third-party notices and attribution

## Tutorial foundations and game assets

This was the author's first Godot project. The entire original shooting demo was built step by step by following [Voidmatrix's Godot 2D shooter introduction](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1UQRwBoEiy), part of the “From Zero to Godot Game Development” series. Textures and sprites were supplied with that guide. Auto player mode and survival waves are the author's original designs, added beyond the tutorial.

The tutorial-supplied assets retain their original terms. Existing audio and font files are also retained from the practice project; their full upstream attribution and redistribution terms have not been verified. These assets are not claimed as newly created portfolio artwork or music, and this notice does not assign them a new license.

## Development assistance

The author directed OpenAI Codex assistance for coding, debugging, testing and documentation to improve development efficiency. The author defined the Auto and survival-wave designs, reviewed gameplay, identified failures and judged changes through gameplay and validation results. Design decisions and evaluation remained the author's responsibility. Assisted implementation is disclosed rather than represented as entirely unaided code authorship.
